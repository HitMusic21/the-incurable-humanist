# Ideal Customer Profile & Growth Analysis
## The Incurable Humanist

**Date:** 19 September 2026
**Evidence base:** Substack first-party data (76 posts, 167 subscribers, 17 months), Google Search Console (16 months), site telemetry
**Method:** Every figure below is measured. Nothing is estimated, and gaps are marked as gaps.

---

## Executive summary

**The publication's growth engine is the Substack network, not search.**

| Channel | Subscribers acquired | Traffic sent |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Substack network** | **108** | 1,133 |
| Direct | 30 | 996 |
| Direct to App | 21 | 481 |
| **Instagram** | **15** | 411 |
| Google | 4 | 338 |
| **Facebook** | **0** | 509 |

Substack's own network accounts for roughly **65% of all subscribers**. Google
accounts for **4**. Facebook has sent 509 visits and converted **none**.

The reader is identifiable and specific: a **politically engaged member of the
Venezuelan diaspora, US-based, who reads serious political and cultural
writing**. Venezuela-themed essays outperform everything else by 1.9× on views
and 2.3× on signups.

Growth has decelerated from **+11.7/month** (first 8.5 months) to
**+3.9/month** (last 30 days).

### 2.3 What this does and does not mean

It does **not** mean the publication should only cover Venezuela. It means the
**entry point** into Denise's work is the intersection of Venezuela, grief,
migration and witness. Readers arrive through that door, then stay for the
rest — the essays on art, grief and New York sustain a 46% median open rate.

Venezuela is the **acquisition** subject. The broader corpus is the
**retention** subject.

## 3. The ICP statement

> **A politically engaged member of the Venezuelan — or broader Latin American —
> diaspora, most likely living in the United States, who reads serious
> political and cultural writing (Heather Cox Richardson, Robert Reich, The
> Free Press).**
>
> **They discover the publication through Substack Notes or a recommendation
> from a publication they already trust — almost never through search.**
>
> **They subscribe when an essay connects Venezuela's collapse to something
> larger: grief, migration, art, or what it means to witness a country from
> far away.**
>
> **They open roughly half of what is sent, use the Substack app, and do not
> unsubscribe.**

### Secondary ICP — speaking clients

A distinct audience with different economics, currently served by `/speak`:
cultural centers, universities, museums, literary festivals, law schools. This
is **not yet evidenced** — there is no booking data. Treat the speaking
audience as a hypothesis until there are enquiries to analyse.

## 4. Growth analysis

### 4.1 The trajectory

| Phase | Net growth |
|---|---|
| First 8.5 months (Apr–Dec 2025) | **+11.7/month** |
| 2026 to date | +6.2/month |
| Last 3 months | +6.9/month |
| **Last 30 days** | **+3.9/month** |

Across 522 days: 110 days of growth, 22 of decline, and **386 days flat**.
Growth is incidental rather than driven.

The single largest day was **6 Jan 2026: +10**, coinciding with *The Night
Hope Returned* — a Venezuela post. The pattern is consistent.

### 4.2 Channel detail

Within the Substack network (108 subscribers):

| Sub-source | Subscribers |
|---|---:|
| **Notes** | **58** |
| Other Substack | 28 |
| **Recommendations** | **21** |
| Substack search | 1 |

**Notes alone has produced 14× more subscribers than Google.**

### 4.3 Channels that are not working

- **Facebook: 509 visits → 0 subscribers.** Either the traffic is
  mis-targeted or the landing experience fails. It is currently pure waste.
- **Google: 338 visits → 4 subscribers.** Not broken, but structurally minor.
- **Instagram: 411 visits → 15 subscribers.** The best non-Substack channel,
  and probably under-exploited.

## 5. Recommendations, ranked by evidence

### 5.1 Publish Notes deliberately — highest leverage

58 of 167 subscribers came from Notes. Nothing else in the dataset comes
close. If Notes are currently incidental, making them a deliberate practice is
the single highest-return change available.

**Leading indicator:** subscribers attributed to Notes, month over month.

### 5.2 Pursue cross-recommendations

21 subscribers already arrived this way, with zero apparent effort. The
overlap table names the targets, and **Carlos Egaña** (8% overlap, Venezuelan,
Brooklyn) is the obvious first approach.

**Leading indicator:** number of publications recommending TIH.

### 5.3 Lead with the Venezuela–diaspora–grief intersection

Not exclusively — but acquisition posts should sit in the territory that
demonstrably converts at 2.3×.

### 5.4 Fix or abandon Facebook

509 visits and no conversions is worth either diagnosing or stopping.

### 5.5 Talk to the readers

At 167 subscribers, direct conversation is feasible and would surface what no
dashboard can: why they subscribed, what they forward, who else should read
it. Ten conversations would likely outperform another quarter of analytics.

## 6. What this analysis corrects

**Earlier in this engagement I concluded the site had "a demand problem —
nobody is searching for this."** The first half was right; the conclusion was
wrong.

Search demand is genuinely near zero: **1 query, 1 impression, 0 clicks in 16
months**, and 2 clicks over six months. But demand for the *writing* is not
zero — it flows through Substack's network, which produced 108 subscribers and
11.3K views in the same period.

**The SEO work was not wasted** — the site is now technically sound, correctly
canonicalised, fully indexed (66 of 76 essays), and readable by AI crawlers
without JavaScript. Those are durable assets.

**But SEO was never going to be the growth lever,** and this data says so
plainly. Effort spent on search optimisation competes with effort spent on
Notes, where the return is measurably 14× higher.

## Appendix A — Methodology

**Tools.** Data pulled via `substack-mcp@1.3.2` (MIT, marcomoauro), installed
19 Sep 2026, authenticated with a session token. Search data via the OpenSEO
MCP against the verified `sc-domain:theincurablehumanist.com` property.

**Reports used:** `get_publication_stats`, `get_post_stats` (ordered by
signups and by views), `get_analytics` for `growth_sources`,
`audience_overlap`, `audience_locations`, `retention_summary`, `unsubscribes`.

**Known limitations.**
- The post sample is 29 of 76, selected as the top performers by signups and
  by views. Medians are therefore biased upward; the *relative* comparisons
  (Venezuela vs. rest) are drawn from within the same sample and are sound.
- Growth-source attribution totals 201 against 167 subscribers, implying
  multi-touch attribution. Proportions are reliable; absolute counts are not
  additive.
- The Substack API rate-limits aggressively. Calls were spaced; several
  initial failures were rate limiting rather than bad parameters.

**Security note.** The session token used is equivalent to full account
access and is stored unencrypted in `~/.claude.json`. It expires 18 Dec 2026.
Remove the MCP server or log out of Substack to invalidate it. Two-factor
authentication is currently **not enabled** on the Substack account.
